Animated Thumbnails bring motion previews to your video experiences. When viewers hover over or scroll past a video tile, a short, silent clip plays automatically so audiences can quickly gauge relevance and decide to watch. The result: faster content discovery, higher confidence to click, and a more modern, engaging video surface across homepages, galleries, and libraries.
